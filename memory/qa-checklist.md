# QA Checklist

## Messaging QA
- Category is stated clearly
- Live products are distinguished from roadmap items
- Claims remain specific and non-inflated

## Funnel QA
- Primary CTA is visible and understandable
- Forms work
- Tracking fires on core actions
- Hand-off from page to product or contact flow is clear

## Technical QA
- Pages render correctly on desktop and mobile
- Structured data remains valid where used
- No broken links across core offers
