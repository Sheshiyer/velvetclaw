# Research Methods

## Priority Order
1. First-party repo docs and brand docs
2. Live product surfaces and product copy
3. Founder writing, essays, and public interviews
4. Audience language from communities, replies, and inquiry messages
5. Competitor and adjacent-category analysis

## Method Rules
- Separate fact, inference, and speculation explicitly.
- Prefer exact source language when it reveals worldview or buyer motivation.
- Track not just what people say, but what category they think this belongs to.
- Flag category confusion immediately; it is a core strategic risk for Noesis.

## Research Questions
- What pain or dissatisfaction makes the reader receptive to this work?
- What language causes them to misclassify the brand?
- Which products are legible today, and which need more explanation?
- What proof signals reduce the "too esoteric / too vague" objection?
