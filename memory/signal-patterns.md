# Signal Patterns

## Positive Signals
- Users ask for a framework deeper than productivity but more grounded than vague spirituality.
- People respond to language about discernment, practice, and interface.
- Narrative content drives curiosity into product questions.

## Warning Signals
- The brand gets collapsed into astrology, journaling, or self-help.
- Users want inspiration without practice.
- Content performs on aesthetics but fails to move people into a concrete next step.

## What To Escalate
- Repeated objections about vagueness
- Confusion between live products and roadmap items
- Strong audience resonance around a specific entry point or use case
