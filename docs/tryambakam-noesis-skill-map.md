## Mapping Logic

- Strategy stays with JARVIS so category, offer design, pricing, and GTM choices are centralized.
- ATLAS owns discovery-heavy skills where source quality and market interpretation matter most.
- TRENDY handles social resonance, trend scanning, and lightweight social ideation.
- SCRIBE owns writing-heavy execution.
- SAGE owns lifecycle, conversion, retention, and relationship workflows.
- CLAWD owns implementation-facing marketing skills that often require code or tracking changes.
- SENTINEL owns audit and optimization loops where measurement quality matters more than authorship.
- CLIP supports short-form packaging once long-form or launch narratives already exist.
